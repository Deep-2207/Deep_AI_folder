Phase 1: Macro Alignment & Regime Confirmation

Step 1 of 5

Price is trading comfortably above the 200 EMA (Bull Market Regime). The 9 EMA has crossed above the 21 EMA and both are sloping upward at a 40° angle. We exclusively look for CALL options; Puts are completely off the table.

⚡ Greek Edge: Theta decay works against option buyers. Never chase green candles at the top; wait for the pullback into the value pocket.
Bias: CALL ONLY
🎯 The Value Zone

The space between the 9 & 21 EMA is institutional dynamic support. This is where options premiums contract, giving you the cheapest entry before the next impulse.

🛑 Defined Invalidation

If a 5-minute/15-minute candle closes across the 21 EMA, the trend has lost momentum. Cut the contract immediately to protect capital.

💰 Profit Target & Trail

Sell 50% at the prior high or 1.5x Risk/Reward. Trail the remaining contracts candle-by-candle along the 9 EMA until a candle closes beneath it.
